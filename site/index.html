See what your goggles see

The DJI Goggles 3 live feed on your Mac, over USB-C. Record it, replay it, send it to OBS.

Download GogglesView 0.4.0 (2.3 MB) Free and GPL-3.0. Apple Silicon, macOS 14 or later. Read the source.

Measured

Camera to screen in about a seventh of a second

Point the goggles at a stopwatch running on the Mac's own screen. The picture in the app shows an older time than the stopwatch does. The gap is the whole delay: camera, air link, goggles, USB, decode, display.

Each reading is good to about one frame at 60 Hz, 17 ms. The app's own share is a few milliseconds of that.

What it does

Recording

Saves the stream the goggles send, untouched

No re-encode, so no quality loss. Files go to ~/Movies/GogglesView as .mov or .mp4.

  • Start by hand or when the stream goes live
  • Split by length or size, drop markers while flying
  • Clip gallery with trim that doesn't re-encode
  • Optional logo or text burned in

Send it somewhere

Five outputs

  • UDP MPEG-TS
  • RTMP, for Twitch or YouTube
  • SRT
  • Local web viewer
  • NDI, experimental

Next release, 0.5

Stabilize, grade, crop for export

Electronic stabilization that adds no frames of delay, .cube LUT looks, and a separate crop for what you record and stream. All in the code, not yet in a download and not yet tried on live goggles.

Control

Without leaving your editor

Menu bar controls, global hotkeys, a gogglesview:// URL scheme and AppleScript. Start a recording from a shell script with open gogglesview://….

Replay

Save what just happened

A rolling buffer you can write to disk after the moment passes.

For OBS and for more than one pair

A window made to be captured

A chrome-less capture window for Window Capture, optional keep-on-top, and a mini window. Plug in several goggles and each gets its own window and settings. The app checks GitHub for updates, verifies the checksum and signature, and installs them only when you are not recording.

Set up in four steps

  1. On the goggles, open Settings, then About, and turn on OTG Wired Connection to Computer. Unplug before you toggle it, then plug back in.
  2. Drag GogglesView to Applications. The first time, right-click it and choose Open, because releases aren't notarized.
  3. Approve the helper in System Settings, General, Login Items & Extensions. It owns the USB device.
  4. Start Share Liveview on the goggles, pick them in the app.

What to know before you install

Only Goggles 3 has been tried
Other DJI goggles may or may not work. Nobody has checked.
Apple Silicon only
The bundled USB library is universal, but nothing has run on an Intel Mac.
Not notarized
That needs a paid Apple developer account. macOS asks you to confirm the first launch. Updates inside the app are checked against the release checksum and code signature.
No calls home
The app contacts GitHub to look for updates, and nothing else unless you set up a stream or webhook. You can switch the update check off.
Something broken
Open an issue and attach the diagnostics report from Settings, Advanced.
